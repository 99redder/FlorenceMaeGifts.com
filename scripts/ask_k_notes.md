## About the shop

- Florence Mae Gifts, LLC is Megan's shop. She was born and raised in Salisbury, Maryland, and has crocheted since she was young; her family has a long line of seamstresses, knitters and crocheters.
- Everything physical is crocheted by hand and made to order after you buy it, so there is a make (processing) time before it ships.
- Many designs are inspired by characters from anime and other popular shows (for example Dragon Ball Z). They are fan-made pieces, not official merchandise.
- Megan also sells on Etsy (https://www.etsy.com/shop/florencemaegifts) and Facebook Marketplace.

## Contacting Megan

## Ordering and payment

- Choose a size on the listing, then Buy Now. Checkout is on the site through Stripe's secure checkout page. Prices are in U.S. dollars and sales tax is worked out at checkout.
- The order is confirmed once payment goes through, and an order confirmation is emailed.
- Megan may cancel and fully refund an order if a listing had a pricing error or she can't make the item.

## Sizing

- The hats are stretchy, like a beanie. In general Megan recommends getting the next larger size.
- For a personal recommendation, send Megan the child's head circumference with the message form (topic: sizing). To measure, wrap a soft tape measure around the head just above the eyebrows and ears.

## Custom pieces and colors

- Custom colors are available on request for listed items: complete the purchase, then send the color request through the message form.
- Custom pieces (a character, theme or item that isn't listed) start with a custom request through the message form ("Request Custom Piece" in the menu). A request is not an order: a custom piece is confirmed once Megan and the buyer agree on the details and price and payment is made.
