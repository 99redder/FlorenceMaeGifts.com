Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

Florence Mae Gifts, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website https://www.florencemaegifts.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect when you visit the Site or place an order, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information We Collect
When you place an order: your name, email address, billing address, shipping address, the items and sizes you ordered, and the amount paid. Payment is handled by Stripe on its own secure checkout page. We never see or store your full card number.
When you use the contact form: your name, email address, and your message, plus anything else you choose to add, such as a phone number, an order number, or your child's age and head size for sizing.
Automatically: like most websites, our hosting providers record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

How We Use Your Information
We use your information to make and ship your order, to email your order confirmation, shipping updates, and digital download links, to answer your questions and custom requests, to process returns and refunds, and to keep the business and tax records the law requires. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who We Share It With
We share information only with the service providers that help us run the shop, and only as needed for them to do their job: Stripe (payment processing and sales tax calculation), our email delivery provider (order and contact emails), our website hosting and security providers, Google (purchase measurement, described under Cookies and Tracking), and the U.S. Postal Service (delivering your package). We may also disclose information when required by law.

How Long We Keep It
We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, tax, and legal purposes. Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a record of your request.

Cookies and Tracking
The Site uses browser localStorage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). We use the Google tag to measure how many visits from Google's free product listings lead to an order. It may set cookies in your browser and, when an order is completed, sends Google the order total and the item purchased. It does not send your name, email address, shipping address, or payment details, and we do not run advertising or sell this data. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, counts page visits and measures page loading speed without using cookies or identifying individual visitors. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page to process payments and prevent fraud. Fonts on the Site are loaded from Google Fonts, which may collect usage data in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

Your Choices
You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us through the contact form on the Site. We will honor your request unless we need to keep the information for legal, tax, or order-related reasons.

Third-Party Links
The Site contains links to third-party websites including Instagram, Etsy, and Facebook. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy
Our products are made for babies and children, but the Site is intended for adult shoppers. The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact form on our Site, or by mail at Florence Mae Gifts, LLC, PO Box 317, Parsonsburg, MD 21849.
